About Me

Tom Pigeon is the pen name of designer, maker and artist Kirsty Thomas. The Tom Pigeon studio was founded in 2014, a space to explore form, colour, materials and process and work across lots of different disciplines. I love the challenge and excitement of new projects and have worked across print, homewares, jewellery, sculpture, paintings, textiles and stationery. I have even designed the occasional garden!

Collaboration is a huge part of my process and since launching in 2014 I have worked with clients, galleries and design stores around the world. I have also been lucky enough to create bespoke product ranges for The Barbican, Tate Modern, The Design Museum, the V&A, Team GB and Johnson Tiles. If you're interested in collaborating I would love to have a chat.

I work with amazing people who are passionate and knowledgable about their craft; small-scale screen printers, traditional foil-blockers, old-school letterpress workshops, family-run metal-workers and independent makers. Our products are created and manufactured with love!

When not working I can generally be found growing vegetables, rummaging around flea markets, throwing myself in the sea or dancing round the kitchen.
